Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VDFY.

Do respondents need to install anything?

No. The recording interface runs entirely in the browser. Respondents simply click the link, record, and paste their response URL. No extensions or downloads are required for them.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. VDFY is fully optimized for mobile browsers on iOS and Android, allowing respondents to record video or audio on the go.

How long does transcription take?

AI transcription and summarization typically complete within 1–2 minutes after the video is uploaded, ensuring your data is ready for review almost immediately.

Is the data secure?

Yes. We prioritize privacy. Only the form owner (you) can access and view the submitted video responses. Unauthorized access is strictly blocked.

Can I collect audio-only responses?

Yes. You can enable audio-only mode, which is ideal for low-bandwidth areas or situations where respondents may prefer not to be on camera.

Is there a monthly fee?

VDFY operates on a flexible prepaid balance system. You pay only for the responses you receive, with no mandatory monthly subscriptions.
